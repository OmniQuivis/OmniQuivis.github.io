   "summary_html": "The video explains LLM quantization download choices on Hugging Face. It clarifies that GGUF is a file format/container used by llama.cpp, MLX is Apple's machine learning engine/framework, and NVFP4 is a 4-bit floating-point number format introduced by NVIDIA in June 2025 for Blackwell chips (RTX 50 series and data center cards). NVFP4 can exist inside GGUF, MLX folders, or safetensors. NVIDIA's NVFP4 builds are mixed precision: feed-forward blocks in NVFP4, attention layers in 8-bit FP8, and embeddings at 16-bit, making them larger than pure Q4 GGUF files (e.g., a 27B parameter model NVFP4 ~21.92 GB vs Q4 ~16.56 GB). Prefill (reading prompts) is compute-bound and benefits from native 4-bit hardware on Blackwell; decode (writing tokens) is memory-bandwidth bound and is similar across formats. On RTX 30/40 cards without native 4-bit support, NVFP4 GGUF reads slower and may drift more from the original model.
